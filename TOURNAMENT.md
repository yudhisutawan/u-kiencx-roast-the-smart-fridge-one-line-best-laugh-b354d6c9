# Tournament — Roast the Smart Fridge

Judge criteria, in the published order: (1) roasts a smart home gadget in one
line, (2) funny — a real laugh with a turn/sting at the end, (3) fresh — an
angle/image/phrasing not met before, not a stock fridge joke, (4) fits one
line with no padding, (5) specific to a real behaviour of the gadget.

## Screening

18 candidates were built (8 Hermes + 10 local). The 8 strongest on criteria
2+3+5 went to the round robin: H2, H3, H5, H6, L1, L2, L3, L5.

Cut in screening, and why:
- **H1** — the rules explicitly warn that a stock joke about a fridge ordering
  too much milk loses; the "existential dread" twist cannot save the stock core.
- **H4** — eco-mode-as-punishment is a familiar trope; sting is mild.
- **H7, H8** — good, but their stings ("out of spite", "deep pity") are softer
  than the eight that advanced.
- **L4, L6, L7, L9** — decent setups, weaker or more familiar second halves.
- **L8** — good twist, but the "life choices" punch is the same device L3
  lands harder and tighter.
- **L10** — funny premise, but wordy (123 chars) and two stings dilute the hit.

## Why L3 wins

- **Funny first:** a fake Alexa now-playing response — "sounds of a man lying
  to himself" — is a sting that lands at the very last word, and it is the
  line a reader would repeat to someone else verbatim.
- **Fresh:** the "now playing" response format as the punchline is an angle
  none of the other 17 candidates uses.
- **Specific:** the speaker's real behaviour — always listening, misreading
  the user, announcing what it heard — is the whole joke, not generic smart
  home talk.
- **Tight:** 98 characters, one sentence, no padding, setup in 9 words.
- **Clean:** no stock fridge-milk joke, no shock for its own sake.
